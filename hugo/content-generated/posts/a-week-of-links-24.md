<p>Links this week:</p>
<ol type="1">
<li><p>The Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization has a special issue out “<a href="http://www.sciencedirect.com/science/journal/01672681/90/supp/S">Evolution as a General Theoretical Framework for Economics and Public Policy</a>”. You can also access the papers through <a href="http://evolution-institute.org/jebo">the Evolution Institute website</a> and there is a series of <a href="http://www.thisviewoflife.com/index.php/magazine/articles/evolution-and-economics-special-issue">summary articles in Evolution: This View of Life</a>. Many look worth a read, and I’ll post about them over coming weeks/months.</p></li>
<li><p>David Sloan Wilson (one of the editors and authors in the JEBO special issue above) has an <a href="http://www.aeonmagazine.com/living-together/how-evolution-can-reform-economics/">article in aeon magazine</a>critiquing economics from an evolutionary angle.</p></li>
<li><p>Also in aeon magazine, <a href="http://www.aeonmagazine.com/being-human/david-berreby-obesity-era/">an interesting take on obesity</a> (HT: <a href="http://johnhawks.net/weblog/reviews/health/obesity-complexity-2013.html">John Hawks</a>). It’s fair to say that a simple “calorie in-calorie out” analysis doesn’t cut the mustard anymore. (But I don’t buy the bit about lab animal food staying the same).</p></li>
<li><p>Another article from Evolution: This View of Life that is worth a look - <a href="http://www.thisviewoflife.com/index.php/magazine/articles/1094/infections-institutions-and-life-histories-searching-for-the-origins-of-ind">Daniel Hruschka on collectivism versus individualism</a>.</p></li>
<li><p>Support for <a href="/posts/social-mobility-across-the-generations.html">Gregory Clark’s argument</a> that analysis of social mobility over a single generation overestimates its extent - a <a href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/education-23101446">child’s socioeconomic position is determined by their grandparents</a>, not just their parents (blog post on this article to come soon).</p></li>
<li><p><a href="http://www.telegraph.co.uk/finance/economics/10155449/Britains-baby-boom-will-affect-our-economy-more-than-anything-Mark-Carney-does.html">Britain is undergoing a baby boom</a>. I <a href="/posts/fertility-is-going-to-go-up.html">expect this will be a common developed country observation</a> over the next decade.</p></li>
</ol>
