<p>Here are three I recently enjoyed:</p>
<ol type="1">
<li><p>Econtalk:<a href="http://www.econtalk.org/archives/2015/11/brian_nosek_on.html">Brian Nosek on the Reproducibility Project</a> - Contains a lot of interesting context about the reproducibility crisis (of which you can get a flavour from my presentation <a href="/posts/bad-behavioural-science-failures-bias-and-fairy-tales.html">Bad Behavioural Science: Failures, bias and fairy tales</a>).</p></li>
<li><p>Econtalk: <a href="http://www.econtalk.org/archives/2015/04/phil_rosenzweig.html">Phil Rosenzweig on Leadership, Decisions, and Behavioral Economics</a> - The problems with taking behavioural economics findings out of the lab and applying them to business decision making.</p></li>
<li><p>Radiolab: <a href="http://www.radiolab.org/story/rhino-hunter/">The Rhino Hunter</a> - I listen to Radiolab less than when it had more of a science focus, but this podcast on hunting endangered species to save them is excellent.</p></li>
</ol>
