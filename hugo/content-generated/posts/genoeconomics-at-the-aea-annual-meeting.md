<p>Evolutionary biology also gets a slot in the session on the historical origins of comparative development:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Jan 06, 2013 10:15 am, Manchester Grand Hyatt, Randle A American Economic Association</p>
<p>On the Historical Origins of Comparative Development (O1) Presiding: HOLGER STRULIK (University of Goettingen)</p>
Genetic Diversity and Ethnic Civil Conflict CEMAL EREN ARBATLI (Brown University) QUAMRUL ASHRAF (Williams College) ODED GALOR (Brown University)
</blockquote>
<p>This paper might be an interesting addition to the debate about <a href="/posts/harvard-academics-on-genetic-diversity-and-economic-development.html">Ashraf and Galor’s paper on genetic diversity and economic development</a>. The potential for conflict due to higher levels of genetic diversity is half of their argument for a hump-shaped relationship between genetic diversity and economic development, and it could do with some support.</p>
