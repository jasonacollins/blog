<p>I have updated my <a href="/posts/economics_and_evolutionary_biology_reading_list.html">economics and evolutionary biology reading list</a>, with a few new additions including John Coates’s <a href="/posts/the-biology-of-boom-and-bust.html">The Hour Between Dog and Wolf</a>, Gregory Clark’s <a href="/posts/the-genetic-basis-of-social-mobility.html">new book on social mobility</a> and Jonathan Haidt’s <a href="/posts/haidts-the-righteous-mind.html">The Righteous Mind</a>. As before, I have been selective, adding only the best books (or articles) in the area. That said, I am always open for suggestions or comment.</p>
<p>When updating the list, I realised I have written a lot of book reviews over the last few years. I have collected most of them together on one page, which you can find <a href="/posts/book-reviews.html">here</a>. It includes a lot of good books that aren’t on the reading list as they are not on topic. It also contains a few books that are on topic but haven’t made the cut.</p>
